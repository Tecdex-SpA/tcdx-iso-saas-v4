## Servicios

- `buildDecisionLedgerEntry`: identidad estable por tenant, recomendacion, contexto intelligence, priority y subject; idempotencia por `idempotency_key`/`correlation_id`; decision humana obligatoria.
- `buildEffectivenessEvaluation`: metodologia `before-after-outcome-comparison-v1`, ventana explicita, before/after, expected/observed outcome, Data Trust y resultado `effective|partially_effective|ineffective|inconclusive|insufficient_data`.
- `buildOperationalMemoryCase`: memoria tenant-scoped con facts, decisions, outcomes, evaluations, confirmed lessons y AI hypotheses separados; confirmed requiere actor humano y razon.
- `createOperationalLearningService`: persistencia append-only/idempotente y audit a `audit_event_log`.
- `searchMemoryCases`: consulta estructurada tenant-scoped acotada; no crea segunda KB ni motor de retrieval.
