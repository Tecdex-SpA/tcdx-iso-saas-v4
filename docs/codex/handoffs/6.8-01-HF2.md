## Idempotency and conflicts

- Missing contract: insert global contract and v1.
- Existing compatible contract/v1: reuse; no duplicate.
- Existing compatible contract with `current_version_id=NULL` or wrong: update contract `current_version_id` to v1.
- Existing published v1: never updated.
- Incompatible global contract or v1: fail fast with explicit `F6.8-01-HF2` exception.
- Duplicate global contracts or tenant-specific same `source_code`: fail fast.
