F6.10-01 extends Knowledge Base v2; it does not replace it.

- `knowledge_documents` is the versioned document envelope.
- `knowledge_sources.knowledge_document_id` is the additive bridge into existing KB v2 sources.
- existing `knowledge_items` and child tables remain valid.
- operational attachments are not automatically promoted to published knowledge.
- F6.10-01 does not implement tenant ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector retrieval or RAG answering.
