# Future Sprint 4 Diagnosis Notes

- document-object links;
- process/operation links;
- chunks;
- semantic profiles;
- accepted/rejected suggestions;
- missing evidence signals;
- classification and usefulness score.

Future diagnosis can evaluate:

- missing evidence by process;
- evidence mapped to active standards;
- control gaps by process;
- suggested actions based on accepted semantic evidence context;
- breach/gap generation with cited fragments.

Human review remains mandatory.
