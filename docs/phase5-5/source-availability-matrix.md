## Paquete 3 consumer migration note

Cumplimiento, cobertura, readiness, riesgo, controles, hallazgos, acciones, GRC Health, ISO Health y Operational Excellence tienen servicios oficiales y adaptador backend en `GET /api/grc/overview`. Las fuentes de Paquete 4 mantienen su estado original hasta la siguiente ejecucion.

## Paquete 4 source resolution

Survey, assurance, loss, continuity, asset and supplier contracts have operational services, endpoints, tests and PostgreSQL evidence. Asset criticality uses `data_elements` as the current tenant-scoped source.


## Paquete 5 completed (2026-07-29)

BI, dashboards, Report Studio, PDF/DOCX/XLSX, snapshots, trends, comparisons, alert eligibility, explanation URLs and lineage URLs consume the official analytics catalog and persisted calculation runs. Missing official runs are reported as source_unavailable; no BI/report formula fallback is allowed.
